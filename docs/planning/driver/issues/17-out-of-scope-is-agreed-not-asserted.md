# 17 — out of scope is confirmed by the principal, not asserted by the map

**Status:** ready-for-agent · **Blocks:** nothing · **Blocked by:** nothing

*Converted from deficit 20, which DRIVER did not answer.*

[`slices.md`](../../../rules/slices.md) § 1: *"Out of scope is a scoping act, not a step on the
route … it never graduates."* The map writes it, and the map is the agent's. The same rule
says: **facts are the agent's job, decisions are the principal's.**

INGEST's map ruled the pairing screen out of scope. It may well be right. But **permanently
deleting scope is a decision wearing a scoping act's clothes**, and the rule hands an agent
the pen with no gate — it was flagged in the report only because the agent chose to.

**Partly answered already.** Ticket 05 put an alignment gate at the front of step 1 and named
out-of-scope as one of the four things agreed there. What is not done is the rule text: § 1
still describes out-of-scope as the map's to write.

## Done when

- `slices.md` § 1 says out of scope is agreed, not asserted, and points at the alignment gate
  that agrees it.
- A map that rules something out of scope without agreement is a gate failure, not a style
  preference.
